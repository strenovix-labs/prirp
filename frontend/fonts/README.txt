LAND RUN CAPTION
A free font from Land Run Marketing, Oklahoma City
landrun.agency

WHAT THIS IS
Forty-five glyphs (A-Z, 0-9, and . , - ! ' " & parentheses) traced letter by
letter from handwritten captions on glass-plate news negatives made
between 1900 and 1916, anchored by two photographs of the Oklahoma land
run. One archivist's pen, the Bain News Service, New York. Nothing was
invented or redrawn. Caps only; typing lowercase gives you capitals.

INSTALL
Mac: double-click the TTF, click Install Font.
Windows: right-click the TTF, click Install.
Web: @font-face with the woff2.

SOURCES
Library of Congress, Prints & Photographs Division, Bain Collection.
No known restrictions on publication. Anchor negatives: "Land Rush,
Okl." (ggbain.02286) and "Land Office, Okl." (ggbain.02285). Full
per-letter provenance is in the specimen.
